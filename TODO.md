State-vs-state (`scripts/service/eles_sssa_generator_set_eval.py`, grouping each state by the exact set of generators it reports SSSA for): `eles/2026-01` collapses to 86 distinct sets across all 4,402 states (every state has an exact-match twin, max group 149); `eles/2026-06` fragments to 4,212 distinct sets across 4,247 covered states (~98.4% of states have no exact-match twin, max group 3) - the same shape of outcome as the topology-column finding above.
